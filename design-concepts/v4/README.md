# سنج

One direction for حساب‌یار 1.0. It does not continue زرنگار، کاشی، نسیم، سپید، اُنیکس، هاله، آئورا، ویستا، or ریتم.

The product question on the home screen is how much of this month is still spendable, and whether spending is ahead of the line that would exhaust the ceiling on day 30. The same line is the tick on a budget and the average-cost line on an asset.

Open `index.html` for the phone (390×844). It starts at onboarding. `desktop.html` is the 1440×900 view: navigation, the month, and a composer that does not cover the list. Themes روز، شب، and ساده switch live from settings. A keypad, templates, filters, review, and buy/sell with a preview all work.

Still frames for a board or a screenshot use `?shot=` and skip motion:

`onboard` `login` `home` `add` `ledger` `accounts` `budgets` `investments` `asset` `reports` `settings`

Add `&filled=1` on the composer, `&filtered=1` on the ledger, `&toast=1` on home, `&trade=sell` or `&traded=1` on the asset.

Type is [Estedad](https://github.com/aminabedi68/Estedad), SIL Open Font License, variable, self-hosted in `fonts/`. See `fonts/OFL.txt`.
